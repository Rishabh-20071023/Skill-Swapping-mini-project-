# SkillLoom

**Learn. Teach. Trade.**

SkillLoom is a peer-to-peer platform where skills are the currency — no money involved. Know how to code? Trade it for guitar lessons. Good at cooking? Swap it for a language partner. Everyone teaches something, everyone learns something.

> "The future of learning is not solo. It's shared."

## Why we built this

Learning today is expensive, rigid, and wastes a ton of talent. Courses cost hundreds of dollars, schedules never fit real life, and meanwhile millions of people have skills worth sharing with nobody to share them with. SkillLoom fixes that by letting people trade knowledge directly — no cash, no gatekeeping, just a fair credit system that keeps things honest.

## What it does

- **Smart matching** — AI pairs you with people based on what you want to learn, what you can teach, and when you're free
- **Profiles** — list what you offer and what you're looking for
- **Chat & scheduling** — message and book sessions without leaving the app
- **Skill credits** — earn credits by teaching, spend them to learn
- **Video calls** — built-in, so swaps can happen remotely
- **Ratings & reviews** — reputation that's actually earned
- **Search & filters** — find people by skill, level, or location
- **Community groups** — forums for people into the same stuff
- **Badges** — small rewards for sticking with it

There's also an AI layer under the hood that goes beyond matching skill-to-skill — it reads context and intent to suggest swaps that actually make sense, and uses sentiment analysis on reviews to surface teachers people genuinely trust.

## How a swap works

Discover a skill → get matched with someone → schedule a session → rate each other after → earn credits → repeat. Every swap builds your profile and opens up more matches.
