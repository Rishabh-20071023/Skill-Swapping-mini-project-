How credits work

  • Teach to earn

    Complete a session as the teacher and credits land in your wallet.

  • Learn to spend

    Credits are reserved when you book and released when the session ends.

  • Contribute to bonus

    Useful posts and shared resources earn bonus credits — look for the +5 toast.

Typical session: 8–15 credits. New members start with a welcome balance.
